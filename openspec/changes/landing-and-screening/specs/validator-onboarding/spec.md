# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines how a first-time visitor becomes an anonymous research participant: the public sequence
from the landing page through the Ilocano proficiency screening, the server-authoritative
enrollment that mints and persists an anonymous validator identity, the browser-local storage that
lets a returning visitor be recognized, and the voluntary-participation and privacy notice shown
along the way.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The public onboarding sequence is landing, screening, then enrollment

A validator SHALL reach enrollment through a fixed sequence: a landing page that introduces the
study and explains the task, a screening screen that asks the approved proficiency question, and
only then the creation of an anonymous validator identity. The screening answer SHALL be collected
before any identifier exists, and SHALL be carried into the single enrollment write.

This order is deliberate and is not an implementation convenience: creating the identity first
would require a second write to attach a proficiency answer, and would leave a participant
half-enrolled — holding an identity but no screening answer — if they abandoned the flow between
the two steps.

#### Scenario: The landing page hands off to screening

- **WHEN** a visitor activates the start action on the landing page
- **THEN** the screening screen is presented, and no enrollment has been attempted yet

#### Scenario: Screening precedes identity creation

- **WHEN** a screening answer is submitted
- **THEN** the resulting validator profile carries both the submitted proficiency and an
  identifier minted by the server in the same operation, and no separate write is required to
  attach proficiency to the profile

#### Scenario: No half-enrolled identity is left behind

- **WHEN** a visitor reaches the screening screen and leaves without submitting
- **THEN** no validator profile exists for that visit, because nothing is persisted before the
  screening answer is submitted

### Requirement: The approved screening question is the only question asked

The screening screen SHALL present exactly the approved question, exactly the approved supporting
copy, and exactly the five approved choices, in the approved order. A submission carrying a
proficiency value outside those five SHALL be rejected before any persistence operation is
attempted.

A visitor MAY decline to answer. Declining is not an error and SHALL NOT be recorded as any of the
five proficiency values; it SHALL be recorded as an absence of a screening answer.

#### Scenario: All five approved choices are offered in order

- **WHEN** the screening screen is rendered
- **THEN** the approved question and supporting copy are shown together with exactly the five
  approved choices, presented in the approved order

#### Scenario: An unapproved proficiency value is rejected before persistence

- **WHEN** a screening submission carries a proficiency value that is not one of the five approved
  choices
- **THEN** the submission is rejected and no persistence operation is attempted

#### Scenario: Declining the question is allowed and recorded as absence

- **WHEN** a visitor continues without selecting a proficiency choice
- **THEN** enrollment still completes, and the stored profile records no screening answer rather
  than a placeholder, a default, or a substituted value

#### Scenario: No proficiency-derived value is stored or shown

- **WHEN** a validator profile is produced from a screening answer
- **THEN** the profile contains no score, weight, rank, or eligibility flag derived from the
  answer, and no such value is displayed anywhere in the public experience

### Requirement: Anonymous identity is minted and persisted by the server

Enrollment SHALL be a server-authoritative operation. The server SHALL mint the anonymous
identifier itself, SHALL derive the creation and activity timestamps and the initial validation
counter itself, and SHALL persist exactly one validator profile.

The browser SHALL send intent only. An identifier, a timestamp, or a counter supplied by the
browser SHALL be ignored rather than trusted, so a client cannot choose its own identity, backdate
its own enrollment, or inflate its own participation count.

The profile SHALL carry no field for a name, email address, student ID, phone number, address, or
social-media account, and a payload carrying any such field SHALL be rejected rather than silently
stripped.

#### Scenario: The server mints the identifier

- **WHEN** a visitor enrolls
- **THEN** the persisted profile's identifier was generated by the server and is not a value the
  browser supplied

#### Scenario: Client-supplied authoritative values are ignored

- **WHEN** an enrollment request also supplies an identifier, a creation timestamp, or a
  validation counter
- **THEN** those values are ignored and the server-derived values are persisted instead

#### Scenario: A payload carrying an identifying field is rejected

- **WHEN** an enrollment request carries a name, email address, student ID, phone number, or
  address
- **THEN** the request is rejected before any persistence operation is attempted

#### Scenario: The stored profile carries no personal data

- **WHEN** an enrolled validator profile is read back
- **THEN** it exposes only an opaque identifier, the self-reported screening answer, the activity
  timestamps, and the validation counter

### Requirement: The anonymous identifier is kept in browser-local storage

The browser SHALL retain the anonymous validator identifier in local storage as the only value
persisted on the client, and SHALL be able to clear it. A stored value SHALL be validated against
the shared identifier format before it is used for anything, and a stored value that is malformed
SHALL be discarded rather than sent to the server.

Local storage holds a convenience token, not authority. Nothing about a participant's status,
proficiency, or progress SHALL be read from it, and it SHALL NOT hold a screening answer, a batch,
or any research response.

#### Scenario: The identifier is stored after enrollment

- **WHEN** enrollment succeeds
- **THEN** the identifier is written to browser-local storage and is the only research-related
  value held on the client

#### Scenario: A malformed stored value is discarded, not sent

- **WHEN** browser-local storage holds a value that does not match the identifier format
- **THEN** the value is discarded and no request carrying it is made

#### Scenario: Local storage is not a source of authority

- **WHEN** a returning visitor is recognized
- **THEN** their profile, including their screening answer and any progress, is obtained from the
  server rather than from browser-local storage

### Requirement: A returning validator is restored without re-screening

A visitor who presents a previously stored identifier SHALL be restored as the same anonymous
validator when the server confirms that identifier exists. The screening question SHALL NOT be
asked again, and an existing screening answer SHALL NOT be overwritten.

A stored identifier the server does not recognise SHALL NOT be treated as an error and SHALL NOT be
reused. The platform SHALL discard it and mint a new identity, because a validator must never be
handed an identifier that belongs to nobody.

#### Scenario: An existing validator is restored

- **WHEN** a returning visitor presents a stored identifier that the server confirms exists
- **THEN** the same anonymous validator is resumed, and the screening question is not presented
  again

#### Scenario: The original screening answer is preserved

- **WHEN** an existing validator is restored whose profile already carries a screening answer
- **THEN** that answer is unchanged by the restore

#### Scenario: An unrecognised identifier is replaced, not reused

- **WHEN** a stored identifier is not found on the server
- **THEN** it is discarded and a new anonymous validator is created instead

### Requirement: The participation and privacy notice is shown before enrollment

Before an enrollment can be completed, the participant SHALL be shown that taking part is
voluntary, that they may stop at any time, and that no name, email address, student ID, or phone
number is collected. The notice SHALL be presented on the screening screen, so it is encountered
before the screening answer is submitted rather than only on a page a participant may not read.

#### Scenario: The notice precedes the screening answer

- **WHEN** the screening screen is presented
- **THEN** the voluntary-participation and privacy statements are visible on the same screen as
  the screening question

#### Scenario: Declining is possible

- **WHEN** the notice states that participation is voluntary
- **THEN** the visitor can leave the screen and complete no enrollment, with nothing persisted

### Requirement: Enrollment failure is reported honestly

When enrollment cannot complete, the participant SHALL be told so in plain language, and the
platform SHALL NOT present a success screen, a confirmation, or a stored identifier for a
validator that was not persisted.

When the failure is that the platform's database is not configured, the participant SHALL be told
the study is not open rather than being shown a technical failure. The underlying error SHALL be
recorded for the operator; it SHALL NOT be rendered to the participant.

#### Scenario: A failed enrollment is not reported as success

- **WHEN** persisting the profile fails
- **THEN** no success screen is shown, no identifier is reported as stored, and the participant is
  told that enrollment did not complete

#### Scenario: A missing database configuration is reported as not open

- **WHEN** enrollment is attempted while the platform's database credentials are absent
- **THEN** the participant is told the study is not open, no technical error text is rendered, and
  no persistence operation is reported as having succeeded

#### Scenario: A rejected submission identifies the field

- **WHEN** a submission is rejected by validation
- **THEN** the message is attached to the screening control it concerns so the participant can see
  which answer was refused
